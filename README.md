I am a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** based in Yangon, Myanmar, specializing in end-to-end web platforms, desktop productivity systems, database modeling, and AI integration.

* 🎓 **Solid Engineering Foundation:** Earned a **System Creator Diploma** (2-year intensive program in IT Engineering & Japanese) at METRO IT & Japanese Language Centre, backed by the **ITPEC Fundamental Information Technology Engineer (FE)** certification.
* ⚡ **Rapid Execution & AI Workflows:** Experienced in modern AI-assisted engineering, agentic workflows, and rapid prototyping—actively shipping weekly builds through my developer sprint group, **Ctrl+Alt+Defeat**.
